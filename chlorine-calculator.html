How the dose is calculated

Liquid chlorine strength is sold as "trade percent": a gallon of 10% chlorine contains enough chlorine to raise 10,000 gallons by about 10 ppm. That works out to about 12.8 fl oz of 10% liquid chlorine, or 21 fl oz of 6% bleach, per ppm in 10,000 gallons. Bleach loses strength as it ages, so old jugs need a little more.
