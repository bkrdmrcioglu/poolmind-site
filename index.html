Support & FAQ

Which test kits work with Poolmind?

Any. Test strips, drop kits (like Taylor K-2006 or TF-100) and digital readers all give numbers you can enter.

Why does Poolmind ask for my stabilizer (CYA)?

Stabilizer protects chlorine from sunlight but also makes it weaker. The right free chlorine level depends on how much CYA is in your water, so Poolmind sets your chlorine target from it.

How accurate are the doses?

Doses use standard pool chemistry formulas and your pool volume. Real pools vary, so add acid in stages and retest before adding more.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Poolmind. You can also restore purchases from the app's Settings tab.
