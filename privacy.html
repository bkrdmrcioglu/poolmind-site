Privacy Policy

Poolmind · Last updated October 18, 2026

Poolmind is built so that your information stays with you. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

Data we collect

None. Poolmind has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive any of your data.

Data stored on your device

The pools, water tests, notes and settings you enter are stored only on your iPhone. They are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Poolmind only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase is active; it never sees your payment details. Apple's handling of this data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on test reminders, Poolmind schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Exports

When you export your log as a CSV file, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

Children

Poolmind does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
